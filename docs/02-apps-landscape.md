# 02 — Apps Landscape: What's Out There?

The apps fall into five groups. Only groups A and B actually sell to consumers.

## A. Digital-first insurers / MGAs (they carry or control the risk)

| Company | Lines | Status / notes |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| **Lemonade** | Renters, home, pet, term life, car | About 2.98M customers at end of 2025 (+23% YoY). Q4 2025 net loss of $21.7M, a 28% improvement. Uses AI for claims and a flat-fee model. [Results](https://www.lemonade.com/investor/news/lemonade-announces-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-financial-results) |
| **Root** | Auto (telematics) | Gross written premium of $387M, up from $332M. Has pivoted toward partnerships and independent agents. [eMarketer](https://emarketer.com/content/lemonade-root-earnings-insurtechs-scale) |
| **Hippo** | Homeowners | Moved away from pure direct-to-consumer toward a hybrid fronting and platform model (Spinnaker). [S&P](https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/articles/2025/6/lemonade-hippo-shares-finish-q2-on-a-roll-90850234) |
| **ERGO NEXT (NEXT Insurance)** | Small business (GL, workers comp, BOP) | Bought by ERGO / Munich Re for **$2.6B** in 2025. Bought the AP Intego digital agency. [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/20/next-insurance-gets-scooped-up-by-munich-re-for-2-6b) |
| **Hiscox USA** | Small business specialty | More than 600k customers. Bought Vouch's MGA and carrier in 2025. [Coverager](https://coverager.com/hiscox-to-acquire-mga-and-carrier-operations-from-vouch/) |
| **Coterie, Pie, Thimble, Corgi** | Small commercial | MGAs that sell through APIs and agents. Corgi is moving to its own carrier. |
| GEICO, Progressive Direct, USAA | Auto, home | The established direct writers |

**Lesson:** the pure direct-to-consumer insurtechs (Root, Hippo, Metromile) struggled with customer acquisition cost and loss ratios, and most added agent or partner channels later. Lemonade is the only one still scaling mainly direct.

## B. Digital brokers / comparison marketplaces (licensed agencies)

| Company | Model | How it makes money |
|---------|-------|--------------------|
| **Policygenius** (Zinnia) | Licensed broker: life, home, auto | Carrier commission per sale |
| **The Zebra** | Auto and home comparison | Commission via its licensed agents, plus clicks and leads |
| **Insurify** | Auto, home, life comparison | Commissions + advertising |
| **Gabi** (Experian) | Auto and home shopping | Agency commission |
| **Jerry** | Auto: app-first broker | Commission |
| **EverQuote, MediaAlpha, QuoteWizard (LendingTree)** | Lead generation | Sells leads and clicks to carriers and agents |
| **Simply Business, CoverWallet (Aon), Insureon** | Small-business digital brokers | Commission |

Source: [Insurify on comparison-site revenue](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/knowledge/how-do-insurance-comparison-sites-make-money/), [The Zebra](https://www.thezebra.com/about/how-the-zebra-works/), [Policygenius](https://policygenius.com/about/).

**Lesson:** these companies already "cut out the traditional broker" by *becoming* a digital broker. They still take a commission. Our pitch has to be better than theirs, not just better than the agent down the street.

## C. Embedded insurance
Tesla Insurance, Toyota Insurance, Shopify and Gusto partners, and travel insurance at checkout. Insurance is sold inside another purchase. Customer acquisition cost is low, but the platform partner needs to agree.

## D. Agency and broker software (what brokers use today)
- **Agency management:** Applied Epic, Vertafore AMS360, EZLynx, HawkSoft
- **Comparative raters:** EZLynx, Applied Rater, Bold Penguin (commercial)
- **Carrier core systems:** Guidewire (PolicyCenter, ClaimCenter, BillingCenter), Duck Creek, Majesco, Sapiens, BriteCore
- **Claims tools:** CCC Intelligent Solutions (auto estimating), Xactimate / Verisk (property estimating), Snapsheet, Five Sigma, Hi Marley (claims texting)

Source: [Damco P&C software overview](https://www.damcogroup.com/blogs/best-p-and-c-insurance-software), [TrustRadius](https://trustradius.com/insurance-suites).

## E. Claims advocacy apps (on the policyholder's side)
Public adjusters, Claimsmate-style claim organizers, and apps for documenting home inventory. This is a small and fragmented group and a possible way in for us (see doc 06).
