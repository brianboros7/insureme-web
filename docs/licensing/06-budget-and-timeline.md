# 06 — Budget and timeline

## Timeline (realistic, from "go")
| Weeks | Milestone |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | Company formed; attorney engaged; DRLP and home state chosen; trademark check |
| 1–8 | DRLP pre-licensing + exam + resident license |
| 6–10 | Non-resident individual licenses (NIPR, often days to a few weeks each); resident + non-resident entity licenses |
| 6–10 | E&O bound |
| 8–16+ | Agency network joined; first direct carrier appointments (the slowest, least predictable step) |
| 10–16 | Compliance manual, AI governance program, disclosures in the app |
| **~16** | **First real policy sold** in the first state; other states follow as appointments land |

## Biggest risks to the timeline
1. **Carrier appointments**: mitigated by joining a network first.
2. **The DRLP's exam schedule**: start immediately; it's on the critical path.
3. **Texas homeowners capacity**: carriers restricting new business; Indiana may launch first.
4. **Name conflict** on "InsureMe": check before spending on branding or filings.
