# 02 — Launch-state requirements: Indiana, Texas, Illinois, Virginia

Fees are **state fees only** (NIPR adds a transaction fee) and are taken from NIPR or state pages checked 2026-11-22. They change; **verify before filing.**

## Business entity (agency) license

| | **Indiana** | **Texas** | **Illinois** | **Virginia** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Regulator | Indiana Department of Insurance (IDOI) | Texas Department of Insurance (TDI) | Illinois Department of Insurance | Bureau of Insurance, State Corporation Commission (SCC) |
| License name | Business entity producer | **General Lines Agency – Property & Casualty** | Business entity producer | Business entity (agency) license |
| Who must be licensed | DRLP with the same lines as the entity; not required to be an officer | At least **one officer or active partner** individually licensed (General Lines – P&C) | DRLP must be an **owner, member, partner, officer or director** with an active IL license | DLP must be an **employee, officer or director**, licensed *before* the entity applies |
| Resident entity fee | **$40** | **$50** | **$150** | Not found; check the SCC fee schedule |
| Non-resident entity fee | **$90** (unless retaliatory) | $50 (verify) | Not found; check NIPR | Not found; check NIPR |
| Renewal | $40 resident / $90 non-resident | $50 (late $75) | $150, **every 2 years, expires May 31** | Biennial; window opens 90 days before expiry |
| Other | Resident entities register with the Secretary of State first | Disclose officers, directors and 10%+ owners; can't file P&C and Life agency applications together | Uniform Business Entity Application | **Register the business with the SCC within 90 days** of licensing or the license terminates |
| How to apply | NIPR, Sircon or paper | NIPR (Texas portal) | NIPR | NIPR |

## Individual P&C producer license

| | **Indiana** | **Texas** | **Illinois** | **Virginia** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-licensing education | Required; hours conflict across sources (20 vs 40). **Verify with IDOI** | Reported as **no mandatory pre-licensing hours** for P&C agents (verify; adjusters need 40) | **20 hours per line**, so about **40 hours for P&C**, with 7.5 hours classroom or webinar per line | Not confirmed; the NAIC model standard is 20 hours per major line. **Verify** |
| Exam | State exam (vendor per state) | State exam (Pearson VUE) | Property exam is 92 questions, 70% to pass; casualty is separate | State exam |
| Fingerprints / background | Background check | **Fingerprints required** for residents (verify) | Background check | Background check |
| Continuing education | Verify (typically 24 hours every 2 years incl. ethics) | Verify (typically 24 hours every 2 years incl. ethics) | Verify (typically 24 hours every 2 years incl. ethics) | **16 hours every 2 years, including 2 hours ethics and law** |
| Non-resident path | NIPR, reciprocity | NIPR, reciprocity | NIPR, reciprocity | NIPR, reciprocity |

**Tip:** CE in your *home* state usually satisfies non-resident states (reciprocity), so pick the home state carefully. Holding one resident license plus three non-resident licenses is normal.

## AI and data rules that touch the license

| | **Indiana** | **Texas** | **Illinois** | **Virginia** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NAIC AI Model Bulletin | Not on NAIC's May 2026 adoption map (verify) | Not on the map (verify; Texas has its own data and AI activity) | **Adopted:** Company Bulletin 2024-08 (Mar 13, 2024) | **Adopted:** Administrative Letter 2024-01 (Jul 22, 2024) |
| Rate regime (affects how fast carriers reprice) | File; effective unless disapproved | **File-and-use** | New 2026 rate-review law (doc 15) | Verify |

The AI bulletin is aimed at *insurers*, but carriers will push its expectations onto us by contract (governance, testing, audit trails, human oversight). Build to it in every state anyway.
