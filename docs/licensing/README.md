# Licensing: becoming a licensed insurance agency

**Purpose:** everything InsureMe needs to legally sell homeowners insurance as a licensed agency in **Indiana, Texas, Illinois and Virginia**, with a licensed human approving the AI's work.
**Status:** research draft, 2026-11-22. This isn't legal advice; confirm each item with the state regulator and an insurance regulatory attorney before filing.

## The short version
1. **A person gets licensed first.** At least one founder or hire earns an individual **property & casualty (P&C) producer license** in their *home* state (pre-licensing course, state exam, background check).
2. **Then the company gets licensed.** InsureMe forms an LLC or corporation and applies for a **business entity (agency) producer license** in each launch state, naming that licensed person as the **Designated Responsible Licensed Producer (DRLP)**.
3. **Non-resident licenses** for the other launch states go through **NIPR**, using the home-state license (reciprocity).
4. **Buy E&O insurance**, then get **appointed** by insurance carriers (directly, or through an agency network or aggregator to start).
5. **Run compliance:** renewals, continuing education, anti-rebating, advertising rules, records, privacy, complaint handling, and AI oversight.
