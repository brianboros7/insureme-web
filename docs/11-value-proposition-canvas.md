# 11 — Value Proposition Canvas

There are two customer profiles: **the veteran homeowner** and **the elder homeowner (and their adult child)**. One value map serves both.

## Customer profile A: Veteran homeowner

| Jobs to be done | Pains | Gains |
|---|---|---|
| • Protect the home and family at a fair price<br>• Get coverage fast when buying with a VA loan (closing deadline)<br>• Understand what's actually covered<br>• Get paid quickly and fairly after a loss<br>• Feel respected for their service | • USAA isn't available, isn't the cheapest, or won't renew<br>• Premiums keep rising<br>• Claim delays (44-day average to payment)<br>• Jargon, endless forms, call-center runarounds<br>• Products that wave flags in marketing but don't deliver | • Real savings they can see<br>• Someone who "gets" military life (PCS moves, deployments, disability)<br>• Fast, straight answers<br>• Discounts and benefits they didn't know about<br>• Confidence they're not overpaying |

## Customer profile B: Elder homeowner (65+) and adult child

| Jobs to be done | Pains | Gains |
|---|---|---|
| • Keep the paid-off home protected on a fixed income<br>• Avoid being dropped or priced out<br>• Handle a claim without being overwhelmed or scammed<br>• (Adult child) Help a parent without taking over their life | • Premium increases on a fixed income<br>• Non-renewal letters (CA, FL, coastal areas)<br>• Hard-to-read apps, time-outs, no human to talk to<br>• Storm-chaser scams, assignment-of-benefits traps<br>• (Adult child) No visibility into a parent's policy until something goes wrong | • Peace of mind: "someone is watching my policy"<br>• Talking to a real, patient person<br>• Lower bill<br>• Family can see and help<br>• Feeling safe from scams |

## Value map

| Products & services | Pain relievers | Gain creators |
|---|---|---|
| • AI broker agent (chat, voice, SMS) + licensed human advisor<br>• Multi-carrier homeowners comparison and binding<br>• Renewal watchdog + automatic re-shopping<br>• Discount finder (age/retiree, mitigation such as a FORTIFIED roof, devices, bundling)<br>• Non-renewal rescue<br>• Claims companion (inventory, guided documentation, deadline and communication log, escalation)<br>• Scam shield (contractor and adjuster verification)<br>• Trusted-contact / caregiver access<br>• VA-loan closing quote | • Shops every renewal, so no loyalty penalty<br>• No call-center maze: the agent answers instantly, and a human answers when needed<br>• Plain language, large text, read-aloud, no time-outs<br>• Verifies before you sign: kills storm scams<br>• Binds in minutes, with proof of insurance sent to the lender<br>• Alerts the family before problems grow | • Visible savings dashboard: "You've saved $X with InsureMe"<br>• Veteran-staffed team; respect for service without government-style branding<br>• Mitigation guidance that lowers premiums and also makes the home safer<br>• Faster claims through organized documentation<br>• Shared family view |

## Fit check
| Top pain | Our answer | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Rising premiums | Re-shopping + discounts + (MGA) lower-expense pricing | Strong, but limited by carriers' filed rates until the MGA phase |
| Claim stress and delays | Claims companion + human escalation | Strong. Unique vs USAA, AARP/Hartford and digital brokers |
| Scams | Scam shield | Unique |
| Wants a human | Advisor line | Necessary. A cost driver |
| Non-renewal | Rescue flow | Strong in crisis states |
