# InsureMe — Product Overview

**Homeowners insurance for veterans and elders. An AI broker that works for you, with a real person always a call away.**

---

### The problem
Home insurance premiums rose about **13% in 2024**. Insurers are dropping homeowners in whole regions. Claims take **44 days** on average to pay. Veterans and older homeowners pay a broker's cut (about 14% of premium) but rarely get re-shopped at renewal, protected from scams, or helped when it matters.

### The solution
InsureMe's AI agent does everything a broker does, and does it every day, not once a year:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Shop** | Compares homeowners policies across carriers in minutes and finds every discount (veteran-friendly carriers, age/retiree, roof and mitigation, bundling) |
| **Buy** | Plain-language comparison, instant binding, proof of insurance sent to the lender (VA-loan closings included) |
| **Watch** | Re-shops before every renewal and flags price jumps and non-renewals early |
| **Protect** | Scam shield: verifies contractors and adjusters, warns before you sign away your claim |
| **Claim** | Claims companion: guided photos and inventory, live tracker, deadline reminders, escalation to a licensed advocate |
| **Family** | Trusted-contact access so an adult child or caregiver can help |

**Built for our users:** large text, read-aloud, voice and phone options, no time-outs. A veteran-staffed licensed team is always a call away.

### Who it's for
- **Veteran homeowners:** about 13M, with 77% homeownership
- **Homeowners 65+:** 79% homeownership, and a growing group
- **Adult children** helping their parents

### Why now
- Homeowners insurance crisis: non-renewals and price spikes push people to shop.
- Agentic AI can now do the broker's work. AI insurance apps went live inside ChatGPT in Feb 2026, and broker stocks dropped that day.
- The big programs leave gaps. AARP/The Hartford isn't offered in CA or FL; USAA doesn't fit everyone.

### How we make money
Carrier commissions (Phase 1, licensed agency). Then our own homeowners product as an MGA (Phase 2), where the AI's lower cost becomes **a lower price** for customers.
