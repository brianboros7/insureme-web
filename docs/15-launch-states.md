## What each state means for InsureMe
- **Texas:** the largest opportunity, and also the largest hail and hurricane risk. File-and-use means carriers re-price fast, so the watchdog and re-shopping are most valuable here. Avoid TWIA-zone coastal risk in the MGA phase at first.
- **Illinois:** consumers are angry after State Farm's 27% increase, and the new rate-review law brings regulatory scrutiny. Our "fairness" pitch lands well. Watch whether carriers pull back.
- **Virginia:** the best veteran density (Hampton Roads) and a stable regulator. Likely our **first state** to prove the veteran channel.
- **Indiana:** a quieter, cheaper market. A good test for elder-focused acquisition and low-cost operations.
