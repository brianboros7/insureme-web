# 01 — Industry Map: Who Are These Businesses?

## How the money and risk flow

```
Customer ──premium──▶ Retail broker / agent ──▶ (Wholesale broker / MGA) ──▶ Carrier (underwriter) ──▶ Reinsurer
   ▲                       keeps commission        keeps commission/fee        keeps risk + profit     takes a share of risk
   └──────────────────────────── claim payment ◀──── adjuster ◀──────────────── Carrier
```

## The roles

| Role | What they do | Who carries the risk? | How they're paid |
|------|--------------|-----------------------|------------------|
| **Carrier / insurer** | Writes policies, prices risk, pays claims. Its "underwriters" are employees who decide whether to take on a risk and at what price. | Yes | Underwriting profit + investment income on premiums it holds |
| **Captive (exclusive) agent** | Sells one carrier's products only (e.g. State Farm, Allstate agents) | No | Commission from that one carrier |
| **Independent agent / retail broker** | Represents the customer, shops several carriers | No | Commission (% of premium), sometimes fees + contingent/profit-share bonuses |
| **Wholesale broker** | Places hard or unusual risks (often excess & surplus lines) for retail brokers who lack access | No | Share of commission |
| **MGA / MGU** (managing general agent / underwriter) | Holds *delegated authority* from a carrier to underwrite, price and bind on its behalf. Often designs the product. | No, the carrier's balance sheet does | Commission + fees, sometimes profit share |
| **Fronting carrier** | Rents its licensed "paper" to an MGA and passes most of the risk to reinsurers | Small slice (about 20% or less) | Fronting fee (% of premium) |
| **Reinsurer** | Insures the insurers | Yes, its share | Reinsurance premium |
| **Adjuster** (staff, independent or public) | Investigates and values claims | No | Salary or per-claim fee; public adjusters take a % of the claim and work for the policyholder |

## Wholesale, E&S and MGAs

- Surplus lines premiums grew about 9.7% through Q3 2025 ([Insurance Business](https://insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/wholesale/wholesale-insurance-brokers-and-managing-general-agents-gain-ground-as-surplus-lines-market-surges-529880.aspx)).
- In 2025, 33% of retail brokers did at least half their business through wholesalers or MGAs, up from 23% in 2024.
- Well-known wholesalers and MGAs (names only; sizes not yet verified): Ryan Specialty (RT Specialty), AmWINS, CRC Group, Risk Placement Services (Gallagher), Burns & Wilcox, Brown & Brown Wholesale.
- About 25 major fronting carriers operate in the US. State National (Markel) pioneered the model; Everspan and Trisura are other examples ([Insurance Thought Leadership](https://www.insurancethoughtleadership.com/commercial-lines/hybrid-fronting-model-reshapes-reinsurance)).
