# 16 — How Insurance Legislation Works & Who Lobbies

## 1. How insurance rules get made

```
Federal: McCarran-Ferguson (1945) → states regulate insurance
                                 │
   NAIC (commissioners) ── writes model laws / bulletins / accreditation standards
   NCOIL (state legislators) ── writes its own models (e.g., Rebate Reform Model 2025)
                                 │  (models are templates, not law)
                                 ▼
State LEGISLATURE ── bill introduced → committee (usually Insurance or Banking & Insurance) → hearings
                     (lobbyists testify) → floor votes in both chambers → governor signs or vetoes
                                 │
                                 ▼
State DEPARTMENT OF INSURANCE ── writes regulations (rulemaking: notice → comment → adoption),
                                 issues bulletins, approves rates and forms, licenses producers, enforces
                                 │
                                 ▼
Courts ── interpret the law (e.g., FL anti-rebating struck down in 1984, then partly reinstated)
```

**Example: Illinois rate review.** State Farm raised rates 27.2% in 2025 → the governor pushed for authority → HB 3799 passed the Senate but **failed in the House by 4 votes** (Oct 2025 veto session) → it was revived and **signed in Aug 2026**. The industry lobbied against it throughout ([ABC7](https://abc7chicago.com/post/bill-regulate-illinois-homeowners-insurance-rates-fails-final-day-veto-session/18112235/), [WTTW](https://news.wttw.com/2026/08/04/pritzker-signs-bills-allowing-state-review-homeowner-auto-insurance-rate-hikes)).

**Ways to influence:**
- Testify in committee
- Comment on DOI rulemaking
- Work through trade associations
- Meet with legislators and their staff
- NAIC "interested party" status (comment letters, national meetings)
- NCOIL meetings
- Regulatory sandboxes and innovation offices, where a state offers them

**2026 note:** elected-commissioner races in CA, GA, KS and OK, plus governor races, reshape appointed regulators ([Ballotpedia](https://news.ballotpedia.org/2026/10/21/four-states-are-holding-direct-elections-for-state-insurance-commissioner-this-year/)).

## 2. Who lobbies, and where they'll stand on InsureMe

| Group | Examples | Typical positions | Likely stance on InsureMe |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Carrier trade groups** | **APCIA** (about $5.8M federal lobbying in 2025), **NAMIC** (mutuals) | Against rate suppression; for tort reform and mitigation funding; flexible rate regimes | Neutral to positive: we bring carriers lower-cost distribution |
| **State carrier associations** | Insurance Council of Texas; Illinois Insurance Association; state federations (e.g. Insurance Federation of PA) | Same, at state level | Neutral |
| **Individual carriers** | State Farm (about $660k federal, Q4 2025), Nationwide (about $670k, Q4 2025) | Company-specific | Captive carriers (State Farm, Allstate, Farmers) may oppose anything that weakens agent channels |
| **Agent and broker associations** | **Big "I" (IIABA)** and state affiliates (**IIAT** in Texas, IIA of Illinois, Big I Indiana, IIAV in Virginia); **PIA**; **NAIFA**; **CIAB** (large brokers) | **Defend anti-rebating laws**, licensing standards, commission structures; fight "unlicensed" digital sales | **Most likely opponents.** Expect pushback on rebating, AI-as-producer, and fee rules. Engage early; consider membership |
| **Consumer advocates** | **Consumer Federation of America**, **United Policyholders**, Texas Watch, Consumer Watchdog (CA) | Against price optimization; for transparency, claims-handling standards, rate review | **Natural allies** on fairness, loyalty-penalty and claims advocacy |
| **AARP (state offices)** | AARP Illinois, AARP Texas, etc. | Affordability for older homeowners. Note: endorses The Hartford program | Possible ally on elder protection; also a competitor's partner |
| **Veterans Service Organizations** | VFW, American Legion, DAV, AMVETS | Veterans' benefits; have insurance programs (Lockton Affinity, USAA) | Partnership channel; careful about competing with their endorsed programs |
| **Trial lawyers** | State trial-lawyer associations | Bad-faith remedies, against tort reform | Mixed |
| **Realtors & home builders** | NAR, NAHB and state affiliates | Insurance availability (deals die without insurance) | Ally on availability |
| **Contractors / roofers / public adjusters** | Roofing associations, NAPIA | Assignment of benefits, public adjuster scope | Our scam shield may draw pushback from bad actors; fine with reputable contractors |
| **Insurtech coalitions** | State innovation and sandbox coalitions | Modernize rebating, digital delivery, AI rules | Allies |

**Scale:** the insurance industry spent about **$172M on federal lobbying in 2025**, 4th of all industries (OpenSecrets via [Repairer Driven News](https://www.repairerdrivennews.com/2026/02/09/federal-data-breaks-down-insurance-lobbying-spends-for-2025/)). Most insurance lobbying happens **in the states** and isn't captured in federal data ([S&P](https://www.spglobal.com/marketintelligence/en/news-insights/latest-news-headlines/amid-growing-issues-with-affordability-p-c-insurers-outspend-peers-on-lobbying-86528923)).

## 3. Our policy agenda (draft)
1. **Rebating / value-added-services reform:** support the NCOIL Rebate Reform Model ([NCOIL Nov 2025](https://ncoil.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/NCOIL-Rebate-Reform-Model-November-2025.pdf)) so we can pass savings and advocacy services to customers.
2. **Clear rules for AI-assisted producers:** licensed agency + DRLP accountability, human-in-the-loop for binding, audit trails. Better to help write the rules than be surprised by them.
3. **Mitigation incentives:** FORTIFIED-style mandated discounts and grant programs in TX, IN, IL, VA.
4. **Elder and veteran consumer protections:** scam and assignment-of-benefits protections, clear non-renewal notices.
5. **Transparency:** support disclosure of loyalty or price-optimization practices.

## 4. How to track legislation
- State legislature sites: TX Legislature Online, Illinois General Assembly (ilga.gov), Indiana General Assembly (iga.in.gov), Virginia LIS (lis.virginia.gov)
- DOI bulletins and rulemaking dockets (TDI bulletins, IDOI dockets, SCC/BOI orders)
- Trackers: CIAB rebating tracker, NAIC model-law adoption charts, NCOIL models, BillTrack50 / LegiScan (keyword alerts: "homeowners insurance", "rebate", "artificial intelligence insurance", "FAIR plan")
- State lobbying disclosure databases: who's registered on insurance bills in each launch state
- Calendar: TX meets in odd years (next regular session Jan 2027); IL, IN and VA meet annually. VA and IN have short sessions early in the year.
