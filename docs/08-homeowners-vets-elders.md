# 08 — Focus: Homeowners Insurance for Veterans & Elders

**Decision (2026-11-22):** InsureMe starts with **homeowners insurance**, focused on **veterans** and **elders (65+)**. This replaces the small-commercial suggestion in doc 06.

## Why the segment is attractive

| | Veterans | Elders (65+) |
|---|---|---|
| Size | About 17.3M US veterans in 2026, down from about 21M in 2015 ([CNAS](https://www.cnas.org/publications/reports/redrawing-the-map)). VA projects about 12.1M by 2048 ([Pew](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2023/11/08/the-changing-face-of-americas-veteran-population/)). | 65+ households are projected to grow by about **20%** from 2025 to 2035 ([Harvard JCHS](https://www.jchs.harvard.edu/sites/default/files/research/files/harvard_jchs_tenure_projections_mccue_et_al_2025.pdf)) |
| Homeownership | About **77%** vs about 60% for non-veterans ([Inman, Nov 2026](https://www.inman.com/2026/11/11/veteran-homeownership-rate-va-benefits/)) | About **79%** (Census HVS, Q1 2025, via [NAHB](https://eyeonhousing.org/2025/07/a-warning-sign-homeownership-rate-declines-to-lowest-level-since-2019/)) |
| New buyers | About 1 in 5 home buyers in 2025 had military ties. 55% of veteran buyers used a VA loan ([Inman/NAR](https://www.inman.com/2026/05/25/nearly-1-in-5-home-buyers-in-2025-had-military-ties-nar-says/)) | Few new purchases; long-tenured owners who renew year after year |
| Overlap | A large share of veterans are 65+ (Vietnam-era). The exact share is **to be verified** from VetPop2023 age tables. | |

**Market:** US homeowners direct premiums written were about **$173B** in 2024, up 13.4% from 2023. The combined ratio was about 99.7%, the first underwriting profit in five years ([S&P](https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/articles/2025/5/premiums-rise-ratios-recede-for-us-homeowners-insurers-in-2024-88879098)). State Farm alone holds about 19.4% ([S&P](https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/articles/2025/3/state-farm-homeowners-premiums-soar-exceed-30b-in-2024-87876514)).

## Why homeowners fits the premise
- **Homeowners is the personal line where agents still matter most.** Independent agents rose to about **50%** of homeowners premium in 2022, from 46% in 2018 ([Big "I" via Insurance Journal](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/national/2023/07/27/732444.htm)).
- Allstate's homeowners chief said in 2024 that "no one has been able to really sell homeowners insurance via the direct channel" ([Insurance Journal](https://www.insurancejournal.com/magazines/mag-features/2024/11/18/801353.htm)). **This cuts both ways:** the opening is real, but so is the difficulty. Home policies are complex, and buyers want advice.
- The market is in crisis, and that pushes people to shop:
  - In California, non-renewals outnumbered new policies in 46 of 58 counties. FAIR plan policies rose 152% from 2022 to 2026 ([Risk & Insurance](https://riskandinsurance.com/californias-homeowners-insurance-crisis-is-about-more-than-affordability/)).
  - Florida's rates are 3–4 times the national average, and Citizens (the state-run insurer of last resort) has more than 1M policies ([KQED](https://kqed.org/news/11980757/state-insurance-chief-tries-to-fix-insurance-market-hit-by-climate-change-rising-premiums-and-canceled-policies)).
  - Elders on **fixed incomes** are hit hardest by rising premiums. This is a plausible inference; we found no segment data yet.

## The competition in this segment

| Competitor | Who it serves | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| **USAA** | Active duty, honorably discharged veterans and their families | The giant: about $36B total P&C premium and top claims-satisfaction ratings. It is the American Legion's "Preferred Provider" ([Legion](https://www.legion.org/member-services/discount-center/insurance)). **Our biggest threat.** |
| **Armed Forces Insurance (AFI)** | Military, veterans, families | Smaller option, often recommended where USAA is expensive or unavailable ([Policygenius](https://www.policygenius.com/homeowners-insurance/homeowners-insurance-for-veterans/)) |
| **VFW Insurance Program** | VFW members | Run by **Lockton Affinity**, a broker ([VFW](https://www.vfw.org/join/member-benefits/insurance-programs)). This is a broker we could replace or partner with. |
| **Liberty Mutual** | VFW Auxiliary, American Legion Auxiliary (via Amwins) | Affinity discounts |
| **AARP / The Hartford** | AARP members (50+) | The only national home insurance program AARP endorses, running for 40+ years. **Not available in CA or FL** ([NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/insurance/homeowners/hartford-aarp-home-insurance-review)), which leaves a gap where the crisis is worst. |
| Local independent and captive agents | Everyone | Trusted by elders. Our real competition for the relationship. |

**Positioning:** USAA wins on price and brand with veterans who qualify. We can't out-insure USAA, but we **can** compete on:
1. **Shopping across carriers.** Including USAA in comparisons where possible, and covering veterans USAA quotes poorly or won't renew.
2. **Advocacy USAA doesn't offer.** Claims support on the policyholder's side, contractor verification, renewal watchdog.
3. **Elders who aren't veterans,** plus AARP members in CA and FL, who have no AARP/Hartford program.

## What these customers need (product implications)

| Need | Feature idea |
|------|--------------|
| Elders want to reach a real person | **Phone and video line to a licensed advisor.** Software does the paperwork, a human handles the conversation. "Cut out the broker" means cutting out the broker's *cost and friction*, not human help. |
| Family often helps manage finances | **Trusted-contact / caregiver access:** an adult child or Power of Attorney can view the policy and get alerts |
| Accessibility | Large text, simple flows, read-aloud, no time-outs. Accessibility (WCAG AA) is required for this audience. |
| Fixed income, rising premiums | **Renewal watchdog:** alerts on increases, automatic re-shopping, discount check (age 55+/retiree, protective devices, roof, bundling, paid-off mortgage) |
| Non-renewal panic (CA, FL, coastal areas) | **Non-renewal rescue:** fast re-placement, FAIR plan or Citizens plus a wrap-around policy explained in plain language |
| Older people are targeted by scams after storms | **Scam shield:** verify contractor licenses, warn about assignment-of-benefits documents, verify the adjuster's identity. Scammers target older homeowners ([NCOA](https://www.ncoa.org/article/what-is-a-home-improvement-scam-learn-8-warning-signs/)). |
| Claims are hard | **Claims companion:** guided documentation, home inventory, communication log, deadline tracker. About 70% of claimants after the 2020 CA wildfires reported problems with their insurer ([AARP](https://www.aarp.org/money/personal-finance/filing-home-insurance-claims-tips/)). |
| Veterans buying homes with VA loans | **Quote at closing:** partner with VA lenders and military-friendly realtors (an embedded channel) |
| Disabled veterans | Information on property-tax exemptions and home adaptation grants (SAH/SHA). Information only; not insurance advice. |

## Distribution ideas (low customer acquisition cost is the whole game)
- **Veterans Service Organizations and local posts:** VFW, American Legion, DAV, AMVETS. Note that VFW already has Lockton Affinity and the Legion has USAA.
- **VA-loan lenders, military-relocation realtors,** and base housing offices
- **Senior centers, Area Agencies on Aging, churches,** and estate-planning and elder-law attorneys
- **Adult children** (ages 40–60) who manage their parents' affairs, reached through digital marketing
- Veteran-founded and veteran-staffed advisor team (a trust signal)

## Compliance flags specific to this segment
- **Never imply VA or government affiliation** in branding or ads. Deceptive military-affiliation marketing draws FTC and state enforcement. Avoid VA-style seals and wording.
- **Elder protections:** many states have elder financial exploitation laws. We need clear disclosures, cooling-off rules where required, and a careful process for trusted contacts and POA.
- **Accessibility:** ADA exposure for websites and apps.
- **Public adjusting needs its own license.** The claims companion must stay on the documentation and tracking side unless we get licensed.
- Rates are state-filed. As an agency we can't discount premiums; savings come from shopping around and from discounts carriers already file.
